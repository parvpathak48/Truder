Who We Are

Gujarat's Most Trusted Packaging Partner

Truder Packaging Industries is one of Vadodara's most trusted plastic packaging manufacturers, operating from our facility at 882/5 Makarpura GIDC Industrial Estate, Gujarat. For over 50 years, we have been the go-to packaging partner for businesses ranging from local retailers and e-commerce brands to hospitals, industrial manufacturers, and FMCG companies across India.

Our Mission

To be the most reliable and innovative plastic packaging manufacturer in Gujarat, delivering solutions that balance quality, functionality, and environmental responsibility. We aim to empower businesses with packaging that not only meets their operational needs but also contributes to a cleaner, greener future for India.

Our Strategy

We invest continuously in modern machinery, skilled staff, and sustainable raw materials. Our production process is designed to minimize waste while maximizing output quality. By fostering close partnerships with our clients, we tailor every packaging solution to their specific requirements and industry standards.

Eco-Friendly Approach

Truder Packaging prioritizes environmental responsibility in every decision we make. We manufacture biodegradable and recyclable packaging options wherever possible, and we're actively reducing our plastic waste footprint. We are proud supporters of India's Swachh Bharat Abhiyan.

Our Commitment

From raw material selection to final product delivery, we maintain the highest standards of quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction. Every batch is quality-checked. Every order dispatched on time. Every client receives honest, responsive, professional service — without exception.
